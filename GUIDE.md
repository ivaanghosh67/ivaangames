# How to play Iron Line

**[▶ Play the game](https://buildwithsumit.com/ivaangames/)**

A guide for new players. No downloads, no account, nothing to install — it runs
in your browser.

![Iron Line](docs/media/gameplay.gif)

---

## The idea in one sentence

Enemies march down the road from the left, and you build guns beside the road to
stop them before they reach the flag on the right.

That's it. Everything else is detail.

---

## Your first 30 seconds

1. **Open the game.** It starts right away.
2. **Click a gun** in the panel on the right — start with the **Pistol Turret**, it costs 40 gold.
3. **Click a green square** next to the road to place it.
4. Wait, or press **Enter** to start the wave early.
5. Watch your gun shoot things.

Congratulations, you're playing.

![A wave in progress](docs/media/gameplay-wave.png)

---

## What everything on screen means

| Thing | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Lives** (top left) | How many enemies can still get past you. Hit zero and the game ends. |
| **Gold** | What you spend on guns. You earn it by killing things. |
| **Wave** | Which attack you're on. They get harder every time. |
| **The dirt road** | Where ground enemies walk. **You can't build on it.** |
| **The flag** | What you're defending. Enemies reaching it cost you lives. |
| **L3, L5** under a gun | That gun's level. Higher is much stronger. |

## Controls

You only really need three:

- **Click a gun** in the right panel, then **click the map** to place it
- **Click a gun you already built** to select it
- **`U`** to upgrade it, **`X`** to sell it

Everything else:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `1`–`0` | Pick a turret quickly |
| `Q` `W` `E` `T` | Pick a robot |
| `Enter` | Start the next wave early (**you get bonus gold!**) |
| `Esc` | Cancel |
| `T` | Chat, when playing with friends |
| `Space` | Pause (single player only) |

## The enemies

| | Who | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | **Grunt** | Ordinary. There are lots of them. |
| 🟡 | **Runner** | Fast and weak. Sneaks past slow guns. |
| 🔵 | **Tank** | Tough. Weak fast guns barely scratch it. |
| 🟣 | **Flyer** | **Flies straight across and ignores the road!** Only guns marked *hits air* can shoot it. |
| 🔴 | **BOSS** | Huge health. Shows up every wave from wave 5. Costs you **10 lives** if it gets through. |
| 💀 | **ULTRA BOSS** | Three of them near the very end. Good luck. |

**The flyers catch everyone out the first time.** They don't follow the road at
all — they fly in a straight line. If all your guns are bunched in one corner,
flyers will simply go around them.

### Robots

Robots walk around and you can send them anywhere. Select one, then click where
you want it to go.

- 🤖 **Rifle Bot** — shoots air and ground
- 🤺 **Blade Bot** — **blocks enemies**, they stop and fight it. Brilliant against bosses.
- 🚑 **Medic Bot** — heals your other robots
- 👥 **Gun Crew** — turrets standing near them **shoot 35% faster**

## You only get so many turrets

There is a limit on how many turrets **you personally** can have standing, shown
as **Turrets 7 / 13** at the top of the screen. It goes down the more people are
playing — the road is the same length however many of you are defending it.

| Playing | You get |
|---|---:|
| On your own | 18 |
| Two players | 13 each |
| Three players | 10 each |
| Four players | 9 each |

This is the single most important rule to understand, because it means **you
cannot win by building more stuff**. You win by making the guns you have better.

If you are rich and really want another slot, buy a **📜 Build Permit** from the
Medical panel. The first costs 3,000 gold and each one after that costs about
twice the last, so it is a way to spend a fortune, not a way around the limit.

## Moving a gun you put in the wrong place

Everybody puts guns in bad spots at first. Select the gun and click **Move**,
then click where it should go. It costs a quarter of what that gun costs to
buy — **10 gold for a pistol**, not a fortune — and the gun can't shoot for two
seconds while it walks over.

The fee is based on the *type* of gun, not on how much you've upgraded it. So
moving your best gun costs exactly the same as moving a brand new one. That's
deliberate: the better the gun, the more a bad spot is costing you.

Press **`Y`** as a shortcut, or `Esc` to change your mind.

## Seeing where you're weak

Press **`C`** and coloured dots appear along the road showing how well each bit
is defended:

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **Red** | Nothing can shoot here at all |
| 🟠 **Orange** | Barely covered |
| 🟡 **Yellow** | Getting there |
| 🟢 **Green** | Solidly covered |

Red patches are where you'll lose the game. Look for them **before** the wave
starts, not after.

The colours are judged against the wave you're on, so a stretch that was green
at wave 10 can turn orange by wave 30 without you touching anything — that's
the enemies getting tougher, and it's a warning.

## When you lose, read what it says

The end-of-run screen tells you **why**, not just that it happened. Things like:

> *23 got past you — mostly flyers (23 of them)*
> *Flyers cut straight across the map and your guns that can shoot up only reach 29% of that line. That is the hole.*
> *2 of your units did almost nothing (Grenade Launcher) — 160g sat in guns that never fired at much.*

It's blunt on purpose. Fix the one thing it complains about most and your next
run will go further.

## 🧠 Smart Upgrade

Click **🧠 Smart Upgrade** in the top bar and the game spends your gold for you,
always on whichever upgrade is worth the most right now. Click it again to stop.

It is genuinely thinking, not picking at random. For every gun and robot you
own it works out how much better one more level would make it, divides that by
what the level costs, and buys the winner. Three things it takes into account:

- **How much road your gun covers.** A gun watching a long stretch of road
  shoots each enemy many more times than one watching a corner of the map, so it
  gets upgraded first. A gun sitting somewhere nothing walks past is worth
  nothing, and it will not waste your gold on one.
- **What is coming next.** If the next wave is mostly flyers it stops feeding
  guns that cannot shoot up, and vice versa.
- **What is actually working.** It watches how much damage each of your guns has
  really done this game, and backs the ones doing the work. This is the part
  that catches mistakes — theory says a gun is well placed, but only the damage
  counter knows the truth.

**It is yours alone.** It spends your gold on your guns. Your friend turning it
on does nothing to your side, and it never touches their guns.

**A word of warning:** it will happily spend everything you have. If you are
saving up for a Build Permit or a Medkit, turn it off first.

## Five tips that actually help

**1. Upgrade instead of building more.**
This is the big one, and the turret limit above is there to force it. Every
level makes a gun **46% stronger**, and it stacks up fast — a level 10 pistol
hits about **33 times harder** than a level 1. Three strong guns beat nine weak
ones for the same gold. Not close.

**2. Build on corners.**
Where the road bends back on itself, one gun covers the enemy **twice**. Corners
are worth far more than straight sections.

**3. Press Enter to start waves early.**
You get **3 gold for every second** left on the clock. Once your defence holds
without you worrying, this is the fastest way to get rich.

**4. Keep some anti-air spread out.**
Flyers ignore the road. If every gun is in one place, they'll fly past.

**5. Buy a Blade Bot before the first boss.**
It body-blocks the boss and holds it still while your guns work. 125 gold to
stop a boss in its tracks is a bargain.

But don't plant one and forget it. Enemies hit **much** harder in later waves,
so a bot that held the line forever at wave 10 gets flattened at wave 50. Later
on a Blade Bot buys you seconds, not safety — keep a Medic Bot near it and
expect to replace it.

## Playing with friends

Up to **four people** can play together, each on their own computer.

**To start a game:**
1. Click the green **🌐 Online** button at the top
2. Choose **Host a game**, pick how many players, click **Create game**
3. Click **Copy invite link** and send it to your friends
4. When everyone has joined, click **Start the run**

**To join a friend's game:** just open the link they send you. That's all.
(Or click 🌐 Online → *Join with code* and type the 5 letters.)

![The online lobby](docs/media/online-lobby.png)

### Things to know about playing together

- **You each have your own gold.** Every kill is shared out according to **how
  much damage your guns did to it** — so if you wear a boss down to its last
  sliver, you get paid for that even if your friend's shot finishes it.
  Hitting something that was already nearly dead only counts for what was
  actually left, so nobody can steal a bounty with one big late shot.
- **You share lives.** If the line breaks, everyone loses together.
- **Your guns are yours.** Only you can upgrade or sell them.
- **More players means more enemies** — about 80% more per extra person. Teaming
  up makes the fight bigger, not easier.
- **The game is longer with friends**: 100 waves instead of 50.
- Press **`T`** to chat.

**Tip:** split the map. Two people each covering half the road beats both of you
crowding the same corner.

## Questions people ask

**Do I need an account?**
No. Just open the link and play. Your progress saves automatically.

**Will I lose my progress?**
It's saved on the server, so it follows you even if you use a different computer.

**Can I play without internet?**
Yes! Download the file `iron-line.html` from
[the project page](https://github.com/ivaanghosh67/ivaangames) and open it. The
whole single-player game is in that one file. You just can't play with friends.

**Does it work on a phone?**
Yes. The board and the shop stack on top of each other, and the board stays
pinned to the top of the screen so you can still watch the fight while you
scroll down to buy something.

**Drag your finger to aim, then lift to place.** The preview follows your finger
so you can see exactly which square you're about to build on before you commit —
handy when the squares are small.

A tablet or a phone held sideways gives you a much bigger board than portrait.
First-person mode is hidden on phones because it needs a mouse and keyboard.

**Why can't I build there?**
You can't build on the road itself, or on top of another gun.

**Something isn't working.**
Refresh the page with `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `R`. That fixes almost everything.
